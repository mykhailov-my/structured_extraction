Python Engineer (offline)

   , HR Specialist at AnvilEight      Харьков · At least a year · English Intermediate

As an engineer, you will be involved in architecting, implementing and delivering projects that are challenging from technical point of view.

You’ll be given real autonomy and responsibility, as well as be able to contribute to offer features and implement improvements.

This role involves the following requirements:
- Strong knowledge of Python 3.x language;
- Django Rest Framework or Flask analog. Alternatively, GraphQL;
- Comfort with AWS/Ubuntu/RDS;
- PostgreSQL or MySQL;
- Celery with RabbitMQ;
- Understanding of API design; RESTful API
- Git and Webpack (basic experience);
- More than one year of commercial experience;
- Experience with React.js will be a huge bonus;

Non-technical Qualification and Skills:
- Excellent read & write capabilities and reasonable spoken English;
- Understanding of Scrum framework and roles in a team;
- Ability and desire to learn things fast;

We offer:
- Free English classes, technical lectures and courses;
- You get 22 working days of annual leave;
- We pay for your illness. We don't need a sick list.
- An ability to plan your working day: you come and go as you please provided that you have enough time to interact with the team and participate in daily meetings.
- Modern cozy office in the city center (Kooperativna street);

About Us:

AnvilEight is Python development outsourcing company that provides custom software solutions and mobile development services to both businesses and individuals.

Today, we are an enthusiastic team of 40 members, looking forward to being challenged and to deliver high impact services to our clients all around the world.

Presently, the main stack of technologies consists (but not limited to) of the Python 3.x, Django, React.js.
We also have a mobile team which specializes in iOS and Android application development.
Our main development framework is Scrum. We organize backlogs and do daily Scrum meetings.
At this moment the company is registered in Kharkiv, Ukraine, and London, United Kingdom.
The head office is located in Kharkiv at Kooperativna street.
