Senior Python Developer

   , Researcher at Luxoft      Киев · 3 years or more · English Upper Intermediate

You will join Data Architecture/Engineering group. We are responsible for company's data architecture and infrastructure, including data ingestion, processing and internal distribution, as well as analytical platforms.

Among other projects you will build out an advanced analytical platforms (implemented in Python) and contribute to its improvement, stability, scalability and new features. You will interact with researchers, portfolio managers, infrastructure teams and data architects.
The role requires great coding and communication skills, disciplined software engineering practices and a thirst for technology.
Responsibilities: please look at the project description
Mandatory Skills:
- Python, SQL
- Experience with CI/CD and tests
- Docker and cloud concepts
- Relational and NoSQL databases
- Experience in building scalable systems
- Development webservices in Python is a big plus!
- System deployment (in and out of containers)
Nice-to-Have Skills:
- Node.js
- React/Angular.js
- AWS/GCP
