Core Competence:
 

  • At least 3 years of professional experience in automation, backend development, or a related technical role, with hands-on experience building and maintaining production workflows.

  • Experience implementing error handling, retries, logging, monitoring, and human approval steps to ensure reliable automation.

  • Experience integrating HubSpot, Salesforce, Airtable, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or similar business platforms.

  • Upper-intermediate level of English for clear and effective communication with international clients.
     

What We Offer You:
 

  • Competitive compensation aligned with your skills, experience, and career ambitions.
