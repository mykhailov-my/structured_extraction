Lead/Senior Python Engineer with AWS

   , Recruiter at Intellias      Киев, Харьков, Львов, Одесса · 5 years or more · English Upper Intermediate

Проект для американської компанії, найбільший світовій виробник шин. Розробляємо платформу, яка забезпечує легку інтеграцію з постачальниками для продажу, замовлення, та відстежування продуктів компанії.

Project Overview:

Our customer is one of the most well-known brands in the Automotive parts industry: an international corporation with over a hundred years of history. We are engaged with European based branch to support their efforts in Digital and eCommerce space. Our team works on cloud infrastructure and solutions that facilitate sales and marketing of their products in the US region (~50 countries).

Responsibilities:
- Work with Client associates from IT and Data Science/Platform teams to build new digital products;
- Work closely with Client data scientists and data engineers to develop digital solutions and industrialize diverse data science applications;
- Help set-up and manage the administration of key cloud technology systems.

Requirements:
- Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree;
- 4-5+ years of experience as a Python developer;
- Strong Python for APIs development and Back-end;
- Experience with AWS;
- Upper-intermediate or higher level of English.
