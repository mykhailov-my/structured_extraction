Itera is looking for a Data Engineer who will work on the big Norwegian industrial IOT data platform provider. Clients data platform aggregates and processes data from millions of industrial sensors. We are looking for an open-minded person, self-going and proactive who confess continuous self-development and able to solve challenging tasks.
