Your role will involve: acquire data from primary or secondary data sources, analyze and maintain big data sets, designing algorithms, ML models. - Work with cross-functional team to drive change in product and cost roadmap through management and execution

- Gather information from internal and external stakeholders to inform definition of existing (and potentially future) product roadmap
- Execute analyses at the interface of technical considerations & work with top management

- Experience and understanding of complex web applications/platforms (serverless, microservices)
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS)
- Knowledge of containerization technologies (Docker)
- Experience with version control systems (Git)
- Experience with LLM models and APIs
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills across engineering, operations and commercial disciplines (English level: Inter+)
