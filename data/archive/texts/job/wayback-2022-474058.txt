This is an application that allows a user to work on the design of their room/flat. They can choose the room type and size, add windows and doors, add all required furniture to the room plan. This application helps to visualize your room and understand what furniture and other staff fit the whole design. Once finished, all of your items on the board can be added to the cart and purchased. There is an option to make HD image with your room design, which you can save in a separate section in the application.
There are also more opportunities that we have, like the development of a separate version for a mobile devices, the reports automatization and 360 Viewer, recommendation engine integrated into the application.
