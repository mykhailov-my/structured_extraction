About U-SAVED Charity Organisation
First and foremost, U-SAVED is a network that aims to federate solidarity actions in order to join those who donate and those who receive. The organization is based on a French and a Ukrainian international NGO created in the first weeks of the conflict and which federate different volunteer movements to help the civilian population victims of the war. U-SAVED was created by humanitarian professionals and Ukrainians who share the desire to act quickly and efficiently in the current humanitarian emergency that is facing the country. It is also the result of the impulsive efforts of Ukrainians who organized themselves to face the horrors of war. U-SAVED catalyzes and applies this incredible energy to respond to humanitarian needs.
