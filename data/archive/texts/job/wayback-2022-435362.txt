About the project:
The project is a job-matching platform for medical doctors and recruiters. The two main actors are Application and Recruiters. The recruiter creates a profile Recruiter, Company profiles, and creates vacancies. The wishes of the application and benefits in the company are also stored in the vacancies. The applicants create a profile and enter their wishes for the new jobs and their own experiences. Certain parameters are relevant to matching. Both can respond to this opinion with a thumbs down. Once both give thumbs up, it’s Match and they can get in touch.
