Project description
Creating and managing music on the go became a problem for many artists. This app was created as a solution allowing artists to play, write, and record song ideas all within one application. Artists can share their projects with their engineers before studio sessions allowing instant access to the audio file, lyric sheet, and recording ideas. When using the app all your files are organized and synced into the cloud keeping all your creative work safe in one location.
