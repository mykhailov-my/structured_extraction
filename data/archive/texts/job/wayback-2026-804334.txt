Position Overview:
We are looking for a Tech Lead with strong hands-on engineering expertise leading agile software development teams. The ideal candidate will define and drive the technical strategy of the platform, guide engineers in delivering scalable and secure systems, and actively contribute to architectural decisions and solution design.
