Junior strong Python Software Engineer
Blackthorn Vision

We are seeking a talented Python Engineer to join our dynamic team. As a Python Engineer, you will be crucial in developing and maintaining our software solutions. The ideal candidate should have 1.5+ years of commercial experience in Python development and strong attention to detail. You will be responsible for turning requirements and recipes into functional code, ensuring the quality and efficiency of our software products. We encourage you to apply if you take pride in your work and thrive in a collaborative environment.
This is a part-time role with the potential for full-time employment after three months.
Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field;
- 1.5+ years of commercial experience in Python development;
- Strong proficiency in Python programming language and its libraries/frameworks;
- Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail;
- Working knowledge of MySQL (ability to write select statements with joins);
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills;
- Upper-intermediate level of English IS A MUST;
- Takes pride in delivering high-quality work and strives for excellence.
Nice to have
- Experience with web development frameworks such as Plotly Dash or Flask;
- Familiarity with database systems such as MySQL;
- Knowledge of front-end technologies such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript;
- Experience with version control systems (e.g., Git);
- Familiarity with Agile development methodologies.
Responsibilities:
- Develop high-quality software solutions using Python and MySQL.
- Write clean, efficient, and maintainable code with a strong attention to detail.
- Can follow and implement recipes of known plays.
- Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve software defects on time.
