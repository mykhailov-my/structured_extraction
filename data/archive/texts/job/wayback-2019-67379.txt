Lead Python Engineer (Django)
Enkonix

If you are a Team/Tech Lead with excellent Django experience and you are seeking for new challenging tasks and interesting projects, this opportunity is right for you!
We are looking for a self-starter who is capable of staying on track and pursuing solutions without supervision.
You will work directly with the US client and will be the main person when it comes to making design, strategic and technical decisions.
The product is a web communications platform that will be built from scratch.
It allows users of social networks (such as Facebook) to place phone calls directly to other users without the need for telephone numbers.
We use the Kanban methodology and the following technology stack:
* Front-end - JavaScript (ES6), Vue.js, SSR, WebSockets, Bootstrap, Sass.
* Back-end - Python, Django REST Framework, Django Channels, Redis, Celery.
* And Git, CI/CD with Jenkins, Nginx, Swagger.

* 7+ years working in software development.
* 1+ years of experience in leading a team.
* Proficiency with Python, Django and Django REST framework.
* Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL, Celery, Redis.
* Advanced knowledge of Pandas.
* Previous experience with Amazon Web Services (EC2, S3) and Git.
* Unit and integration testing expertise.
* Upper-Intermediate English.
* Excellent communications, organizational, and problem-solving skills.
* In-depth understanding of web applications architecture.
* Previous experience of working directly with a foreign client is a plus.
Responsibilities:
* Build a new development team from scratch and provide strong technical guidance to them.
* Liaise with the client on aligning and reviewing a product roadmap, its vision, and project scope.
* Lead on architecture design, development, and technology choices to ensure the app’s high performance.
* Manage the team on a daily basis including distributing tasks and helping to solve the tech issues.
The office is located near m. Universitet.
