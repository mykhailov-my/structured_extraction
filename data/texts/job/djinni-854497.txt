Senior WordPress Developer
Intetics

Intetics Inc., a leading American technology company specializing in custom software application development, distributed professional teams creation, software product quality assessment, and “all-things-digital” solutions, is looking for a Senior WordPress Developer to join its innovative team.
Project Responsibilities
Build and maintain custom themes, plugins, and Gutenberg blocks across a portfolio of high-traffic, multilingual WordPress sites
Develop and maintain libraries, block collections, Composer packages, and tooling that accelerate delivery across teams
Own quality and performance: refactor legacy code, optimize queries and caching, and improve Core Web Vitals metrics
Build and consume REST APIs and integrations with internal and third-party systems
Take part in code reviews, CI/CD improvements, and engineering standards initiatives
Collaborate with product, marketing, SEO, content, and QA teams on new features and site improvements
Document technical decisions and contribute to engineering best practices
Ensure software development best practices and timely product delivery
Candidate's Portrait
We're looking for a Senior WordPress Developer who treats WordPress as a modern engineering platform rather than a traditional CMS. You'll help build and scale high-traffic multilingual web platforms while maintaining high standards of performance, security, scalability, and code quality. The ideal candidate cares about architecture as much as feature delivery and writes code that other developers are happy to maintain.
