PR Manager
ClickDealer

ClickDealer - an award-winning global marketing agency - is looking for a talented PR Manager to join our in-house PR team. If you love turning company news, partnerships, and industry presence into compelling stories, know how to build relationships with media and event organizers, and can juggle creative ideas with hands-on logistics, we want to meet you!
More about us: clickdealer.com
Key Requirements:
- English proficiency at Upper-Intermediate level or higher;
- Previous experience in marketing or PR will be considered an advantage;
- Strong communication and presentation skills;
- Knowledge of the affiliate/performance marketing industry and ability to track key trends;
- Experience with media relations, partnerships, or content placement is a plus;
- Creative thinking and problem-solving skills;
- Ability to work under pressure and handle multiple tasks simultaneously;
- High level of organization, responsibility, and attention to detail.
Your Future Responsibilities:
- Generate and evaluate PR ideas around company news, bonus programs, offers, partnerships, and market trends;
- Track relevant affiliate marketing resources, awards, rankings, and directories, and manage the company's participation in them;
- Negotiate media placements, partnerships, cross-promotions, and content exchanges;
- Interview internal and external experts and turn their expertise into publishable content;
- Create and coordinate technical briefs/tasks for the product and design teams;
- Research relevant industry events and conferences for company participation;
- Communicate with organizers and negotiate agreements - passes, booths, sponsorships;
- Coordinate the annual team look design and manufacturing process;
- Research and produce branded merch, including business cards, t-shirts, and other items for each conference;
- Prepare financial reporting on event and merch budgets;
- Manage the New Year gift cycle for partners: research (summer), production (autumn), and logistics (winter).
What We Offer:
- Work with a global brand in a dynamic international company;
- Competitive salary (linked to USD), paid vacations and sick leaves, and medical insurance;
- Partial coverage of English language courses and gym expenses;
- Exciting projects and participation in corporate trainings and seminars.
Ready to elevate your career and embark on an exciting journey with ClickDealer? Apply now and become part of our global success story!
